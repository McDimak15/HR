Homeward Ragnarok
Homeward Journey add-on to Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok

The official DLC for Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok, which adds enhanced compatibility with Homeward Journey.

This mod includes compatibility support for the following mods:
- Homeward Journey
- Calamity
- Thorium

Some limited support for:
- Secrets of the Shadows
- Consolaria

Created by:
Developer: Kes.
Sprites: Duwop, Soltan.
Balance Changes: Mr.Puzzles, Soltan.
Story: targetson.
Localisation: Pudd1ng, De1tA (Chinese), Mr.Puzzles (Russian).
