[h1] Homeward Ragnarok [/h1]
[h2] Homeward Journey add-on to Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok [/h2]

The official DLC for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3456517686]Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok[/url], which adds enhanced compatibility with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2930931197]Homeward Journey[/url]

This mod includes compatibility support for the following mods:
[list]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2930931197]Homeward Journey[/url]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2824688072]Calamity[/url]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2909886416]Thorium[/url]
[/list]

Some limited support for:
[list]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2843112914]Secrets of the Shadows[/url]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2864843929]Consolaria[/url]
[/list]

The mod has been tested in multiplayer, but some bugs might still appear.
It may also be a bit unbalanced in some situations.
